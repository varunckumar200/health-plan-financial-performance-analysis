Health Plan Financial Performance Analysis

A synthetic healthcare financial analysis project using Power BI and Google Sheets to evaluate financial performance across Medicaid, Commercial, and Medicare Advantage lines of business.

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Google Sheets

Key analyses:

  • Medical Loss Ratio (MLR) by line of business
  • Claims PMPM and year-over-year growth
  • Data preparation and validation checks
  • Independent calculation checks and methodology documentation

Data disclaimer: All data is synthetic and illustrative. It does not represent actual insurer or member experience.
